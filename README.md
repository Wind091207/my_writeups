## 🧠 My Learning Philosophy

One of my biggest inspirations is **Xeno Kovah**, especially his lectures on Assembly, Computer Architecture, and Reverse Engineering through OpenSecurityTraining.

One lesson that has stayed with me is the importance of understanding the bigger picture rather than getting lost in every single Assembly instruction.

Reverse Engineering is not just about reading Assembly code.

It's about asking the right questions:

- **What does this program actually do?**
- **Why does it behave this way?**
- **How does its control flow work?**
- **What happens if we change the input?**
- **How can we understand the logic behind the binary?**

Sometimes, understanding why a program takes a particular path is more important than understanding every instruction along that path.

My goal is to keep learning, questioning, experimenting, and gradually developing a deeper understanding of how software works.
